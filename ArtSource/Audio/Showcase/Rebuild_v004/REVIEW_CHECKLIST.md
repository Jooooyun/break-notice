# v004 listening checklist

Status: `HUMAN OWNER REVIEW REQUIRED`

Run the review in order: Phase 1 T3 blind, Phase 2 classification blind, then Phase 3
evidence/detail. Use laptop speakers first, at one fixed OS volume. A public
reviewer records answers in `LISTENING_RESPONSE_TEMPLATE.md` and returns it to
the Owner; that reviewer does not need either private key. The Owner opens the
repository-only keys only after the answers are locked.

1. In the Phase-1 package, play `T3_X_PAIR_COMMON_VIDEO_REVIEW.mp4` and
   `T3_Y_PAIR_COMMON_VIDEO_REVIEW.mp4`. The video streams are identical and
   each file contains two variations. If MP4 playback is unavailable, use the
   matching X/Y WAV files. Pick X or Y for better contact, chair/body
   separation, and tail. Record the laptop choice first, then repeat X/Y on
   headphones and lock one final choice. Send it to the Owner; do not request
   the mapping yet.
2. In the Phase-2 package, play `TIER_10_TRIAL_BLIND_REVIEW.wav`. Write T1,
   T2, or T3 for trials 1–10.
   Pass requires at least 8/10 against `LADDER_QUIZ_MAPPING_PRIVATE.json`.
3. Play `MATERIAL_10_TRIAL_BLIND_REVIEW.wav`. Write Hand, Binder, or Chair for
   trials 1–10. Pass requires at least 8/10.
4. Return the locked Phase-1 and Phase-2 answers to the Owner. Do not receive
   the Phase-3 package before those answers are fixed.
5. In Phase 3, the Owner scores the private keys and reveals the T3 mapping.
   The lossless RC passes only if it is not worse than the approved Z pair.
6. Play `IMPACT_LADDER_ACTUAL_DYNAMIC_REVIEW.wav` and confirm that T1 is
   clearly smallest, T2 is the normal strong ceiling, and T3 is unmistakably
   largest without becoming a cinematic boom. Use the synchronized MP4 when a
   visual review is useful.
7. Play `T1_HAND_12_REPEAT_STRESS_REVIEW.wav`. Reject if it becomes a branded
   click machinegun or if all three variations collapse into one sound.
8. Repeat the actual-dynamic ladder and T1 stress reel on
   headphones. Reject a headphone-only harsh click, smeared chair/body
   separation, excessive low boom, or a tail that masks the next cue. Do not
   reverse a clear laptop hierarchy merely because headphones reveal more bass.
9. Spot-check the 11 individual composites and, when revising, solo the five
   aligned stems. Reject any voice/grunt, magic or
   firearm character, bass boom masking the contact, or missing material.

The public reviewer returns one recommendation only after all audible checks:
`PASS LADDER`, `PASS WITH NOTES`, or `REJECT LADDER`. The Owner records the
official review result after scoring. This pack is not FINAL and does not
authorize Runtime hookup.
