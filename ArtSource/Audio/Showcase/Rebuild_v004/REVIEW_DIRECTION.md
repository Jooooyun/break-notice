# Arcade Edge impact ladder v004

Status: `REVIEW_CANDIDATE / NOT FINAL / NOT RUNTIME-INTEGRATED`

This pack rebuilds the approved Z / Arcade Edge language from new lossless
source bytes. The reusable signature is a fast transient and a short branded
mechanical click, while the physical emitter and Runner body remain readable.
It does not imitate or sample any commercial fighting game.

The exact ladder is:

- T1 Hand -> Runner: 3 true composites. Weak air snap, no brand click.
- T2 Hook -> Runner: 3 true composites. Strong snap, restrained click.
- T2 Binder -> Runner: 3 true composites. Board/paper/ring identity.
- T3 Chair -> Runner RC: 2 true composites. Full snap + click and the largest
  physical mass.

Every cue is 48 kHz, 24-bit PCM stereo, 1.35 seconds, with contact at 0.200
seconds. Five aligned stems are supplied: `pre`, `signature`, `emitter`,
`runner`, `floor_tail`. No voice, grunt, breath, mixer, gameplay hook, fall
semantic, or Runtime component is included.

Human listening still owns the following verdicts: laptop tier/material
recognition at least 8/10, no click-machinegun fatigue in the 12-repeat T1 reel,
and the T3 lossless RC not sounding worse than the approved Z reference.
