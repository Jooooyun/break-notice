## Providers used

- LaSonothèque / Joseph SARDIN: official asset pages identify the files as CC0 public-domain audio. Used for a hand-truck roll proxy, two different 10 kg floor-load recordings, and two non-overlapping takes from a nine-take canvas-bag recording.
- OpenGameArt / BMacZero: the official Mechanical Sounds page identifies the individual WAV files as CC0. Used only as low-level mechanical material proxies.
- OpenGameArt / OwlishMedia: the official Sound Effects Pack page identifies the archive as CC0. Only the previously unused blanket-movement members 2 and 5 are used as cloth/body proxies.

All production inputs are lossless WAV. MP3, OGG/Vorbis, preview audio, login-gated originals, mixed-license archives, and provenance-ambiguous files are rejected.
