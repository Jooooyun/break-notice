Do not open Phase 2 or Phase 3 before the Phase 1 answer is written and locked.

## Phase 1 — pair direction

1. Extract `BREAK_NOTICE_T3_V005_PAIR_BLIND_PHASE1.zip` only.
2. Use the two MP4 files first. They have the same picture; only audio differs.
3. Listen on headphones at a comfortable fixed volume, alternating the pair at least six times.
4. Repeat on laptop speakers without changing the system volume between labels.
5. Write the free response before viewing any technical evidence.
6. Force a choice: which label has the more grounded and heavier Chair → Runner impact?
7. Mark whether either label sounds light, upbeat, toy-like, hollow, muffled, generic, harsh, or tiring.
8. Lock the answer text before opening Phase 2.

## Phase 2 — variation independence

1. Extract `BREAK_NOTICE_T3_V005_VARIATION_BLIND_PHASE2.zip` only.
2. Review v01 comparison and v02 comparison independently.
3. Use each comparison's WAV or MP4 pair; do not compare filename order across v01 and v02.
4. For each variation, choose the more grounded label and issue an independent `PASS` or `FAIL` for the v005 candidate after deblind.
5. Lock both answers before opening Phase 3.

## Phase 3 — deblind and diagnosis

1. Extract `BREAK_NOTICE_T3_V005_DEBLIND_DETAIL_PHASE3.zip` only after both earlier answers are locked.
2. Read the private mapping and compare the locked answers with candidate/reference identity.
3. If v005 loses either variation, overall T3 v005 is `FAIL`.
4. Open the two composites and then the five stems for the failed variation to identify whether the problem is `pre`, `signature`, `emitter`, `runner`, or `floor_tail`.
5. Use metrics only to explain a listening result. Never reverse a Human listening FAIL because automated QA passed.

## Immediate FAIL conditions

- v005 is again light, upbeat, anime-like, toy-like, or generic.
- a contact spike is large but the following body is absent.
- pre or a secondary rattle competes with the main contact.
- Chair identity is an isolated click, free metal ring, or generic cinematic boom.
- either v01 or v02 fails.
- headphones reveal harshness or repeat fatigue.
- laptop speakers lose the physical body entirely.

## PASS condition

Both v005 variations must independently preserve a readable `Chair material → Runner body/cloth → Floor support` causal order on headphones and laptop speakers, without copying Y bytes. Only the Human Owner can issue that PASS.
